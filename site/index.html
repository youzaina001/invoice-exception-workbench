People and hosted scope

The Operator investigates an invoice and resolves its case. An invited Reviewer acts as the Operator in a private hosted Demo Workspace. The Maintainer owns that demonstration and can inspect every workspace. Each Demo Attempt is one walkthrough with its own submissions, cases, checks and audit evidence.

The hosted CSV design resumes the current attempt when a Reviewer returns. Start fresh creates an empty attempt and leaves previous attempts read-only. A workspace keeps five attempts; a sixth removes the oldest. See the hosted scope and its verification limits. Hosted PDF routes remain disabled.
